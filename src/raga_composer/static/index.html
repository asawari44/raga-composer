How to read the notation
  • S r R g G m M P d D n N: lowercase is komal, M is tivra Ma. You can also type p for P and ni for n.
  • S': taar (upper) octave. N.: mandra (lower) octave.
  • _: hold the previous note for one more slot. -: rest.
  • Click सा रे ग to show notes in Devanagari, as in Bhatkhande notation: रे॒ (a line below) is komal, म॑ (a stroke above) is tivra Ma, ऩि (a dot below) is mandra, सां (a dot above) is taar, and ऽ holds the note. You still type with the Latin letters.
  • In a composition, each box is one beat of the taal. X marks sam, 0 marks khali, and numbers mark the other talis.

Compositions

Published compositions with notation. Read the words first, then follow the notation line by line. Each box is one beat of the taal, with the words under the notes.

Performance options

Taans are 8 or 16 beats. Before each taan, you sing from the mukhda through sam, and the taan lands back on the mukhda.

Choose a raga to see its compositions.

Practice alaap and taan

Listen to a phrase, sing it back, then move to the next one. Sthayi phrases stay low and resolve to Sa; antara phrases rise to taar Sa. Alaaps are free and unmetered; each box is one note and its held length. In taans, each box is one beat of the taal chosen in the sound bar.
