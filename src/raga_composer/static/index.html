How to read the notation
  • S r R g G m M P d D n N: lowercase is komal, M is tivra Ma. You can also type p for P and ni for n.
  • S': taar (upper) octave. N.: mandra (lower) octave.
  • _: hold the previous note for one more slot. -: rest.
  • In a composition, each box is one beat of the taal. X marks sam, 0 marks khali, and numbers mark the other talis.
