## Hacker News Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Riyaaz your way, a free browser app for Hindustani raga practice

**Post:**

Hi HN,

I built Riyaaz your way, a free and open source web app for composing and practicing Hindustani classical music:

https://asawari44.github.io/raga-composer/

The app runs as a static site in the browser. It has no accounts and no server.

What you can do:

- Browse raga facts such as thaat, aaroh, avroh, pakad, vadi, samvadi, time, and varjit swaras.
- Read and play swara notation for compositions where data are available.
- Generate and arrange raga-true practice phrases, including alaap and taan material.
- Practice with tanpura, tabla theka, piano voices, and harmonium voices through Web Audio.
- Save work locally in the browser and export it when needed.

The app is meant to support practice and teaching. It is not a substitute for a guru or a source of final authority on repertoire. I would value feedback from musicians, teachers, learners, and Web Audio developers, especially on notation, raga data, and the static app experience.

Source code: https://github.com/asawari44/raga-composer
